In home environments, how a robot moves matters just as much as what it does. Speed and precision directly affect safety, trust, and usability.

## The Importance of Controlled Movement

Home robots operate near people, pets, and fragile objects.

### Precision Enables Safety

Accurate motion allows robots to:

- Handle objects carefully
- Navigate tight spaces
- Avoid collisions

Even small movement errors can break user confidence.

### Speed Shapes Perception

Robots that respond too slowly feel ineffective. Robots that move too fast feel unsafe. The right balance creates comfort.

## Engineering for Predictability

Smooth, predictable motion helps users anticipate behavior. When people can guess what a robot will do next, interactions feel safer and more natural.

Consistency, not raw speed, defines a good home robotics experience.
