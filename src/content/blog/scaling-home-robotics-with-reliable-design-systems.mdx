Scaling home robotics isn’t just about adding features. It’s about growing without breaking what already works.

## Why Scalability Is Hard in Robotics

Every new capability increases system complexity. Without structure, systems become fragile.

### Modular Design as a Foundation

Modularity allows teams to evolve parts independently:

- Hardware components can be upgraded safely
- Software features can be added without regressions

This reduces risk and accelerates iteration.

## Maintaining Consistency Over Time

Users expect robots to behave consistently across updates. Reliable design systems ensure improvements feel familiar rather than disruptive.

Scalability succeeds when growth feels stable, not chaotic.
