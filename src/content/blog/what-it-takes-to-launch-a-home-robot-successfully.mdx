Launching a home robot is as much a product challenge as a technical one. Once a robot enters someone’s home, expectations rise quickly.

## Why Home Robot Launches Are Unique

Unlike software, hardware failures are physical and personal. Trust is harder to rebuild once lost.

### Testing in Real Homes

Real environments reveal issues that labs cannot. Testing with actual users leads to safer defaults and simpler interactions.

## Reliability Over Ambition

At launch, consistency matters more than innovation. Users prefer fewer features that work reliably over complex systems that fail unpredictably.

## Launch as a Long-Term Commitment

A successful launch prepares teams for what comes next: support, updates, and continuous learning.

Home robots succeed when teams treat launch not as an ending, but as the beginning of a long relationship with users.
